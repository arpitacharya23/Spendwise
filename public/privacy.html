Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026 | Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SpendWise ("we", "our", or "us"), available at https://app.arpitacharya.com. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you use our application and services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide when using SpendWise, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, profile picture (if provided via authentication).
  • Financial Records: Transaction logs, expense amounts, custom categories, accounts, payment methods, budget targets, and loan/debt amortization records.
  • Splitwise & Shared Balances: Group names, member emails, shared expense allocations, and settlement entries.
  • Categorization Rules: Custom keywords and merchant matching criteria created by you.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for core app functionality:

  • To display and organize your personal expenses, budgets, and cash flow analytics.
  • To calculate debt balances, EMI repayments, and Splitwise expense shares.
  • To synchronize your records securely across your devices.
  • To communicate critical account notices and verification updates.

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal or financial data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Google User Data & API Limited Use Policy

SpendWise's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. If you choose to sign in or connect with Google services:

  • We only request permissions strictly required to authenticate your identity or sync user-requested spreadsheets.
  • We do not use Google user data to train generalized AI/ML models without explicit consent.
  • We do not share your Google data with external third parties except as needed to provide the app service.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) in transit and secure encrypted database storage. Sensitive numbers and balance metrics can additionally be masked on-screen using our built-in client Privacy Mode. Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may at any time:

  • Export your entire transaction ledger and rule sets via CSV.
  • Edit or delete any transaction, account, category, or group.
  • Request permanent account deletion and erasure of all associated database entries by contacting us.
